# Human-agent baseline

**Not measured.** This project did not time a human agent making these calls, so no comparison figure is given. Any number here would be an invented estimate.

To produce a real baseline, time one person through the same ten scenarios with a phone, a customer record sheet, and the same decision policy. Record the time per call, the wrap-up time, and any retries they needed. Then add the results here, labelled with who ran them and when.

For reference, the agent's recorded calls lasted between 13 and 62 seconds, with most between 35 and 55 seconds. These are Twilio call durations, not total handling time, and they exclude the trial-account message and any setup.
